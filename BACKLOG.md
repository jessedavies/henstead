# Come By: backlog

Ideas parked while the core herding loop is tuned. Rough priority order within each group.

## Done in build 2
- Night choice: leave eggs to hatch or keep them in the basket. Rooster (6 eggs) required for hatching. Bigger coop upgrade.
- Rooster: hens stay near him, he crows at predators, a second one fights and is traded at night for 4 eggs.
- Fox on screen at dusk from day 3.
- Clearing payoff: every 3 bushes adds an egg a night, capped at one per laying hen.

## Next (extends the current loop)
- Snake: goes for eggs in the nest, has to be spotted.
- Feed planting: sown patches lure hens (a herding tool) and raise laying.
- Hawk cover: hens near trees or a covered run cannot be targeted, which makes clearing land a trade-off.
- More things to spend eggs on, so the basket is a harder choice.
- Gather sends the dog into the bushes when the flock is spread near the edge; orbit should stay on open ground.

## Land and building
- Tree clearing: needs an upgrade (the dog can only dig bushes).
- Fence building: place fence runs to funnel hens or protect forage.
- Coop upgrades: capacity, snake-proof floor, covered run.
- Larger map revealed in rings as land is cleared; regrowth of bushes over time.

## Feel and presentation
- Authored low-poly models and animation in place of primitives.
- Recorded whistle and bark sounds, ambient farm audio, music.
- Save progress between sessions.
- Touch: virtual stick as an alternative to tap-and-drag.
- Farmer character who gives the whistles; pure whistle-command mode as a hard mode.
- Named hens with personalities beyond the single rogue.

## Tuning questions to answer by playtest
- Is Gather too strong on small flocks, too weak on large ones?
- Is Steady used at all, or is running always good enough?
- Is the gate homing pull (6 units) too forgiving?
- Is 30s of daytime the right length once there is more to do in it?

# Henstead (cosy farm version): backlog

Built: farmer (KayKit Ranger, animated) with a border collie and four whistles; day and night with no fail state; laying rule (fed, housed, calm); feed patches; clearing bushes, trees and rocks; free placement of fences, gates, coops and 10 decorations; buying hens and a rooster; hatching; named hens with mood cards and status icons; gentle hawk, fox and snake; an 11-step to-do list with an arrow and pulsing buttons; fence-aware pathfinding for hens; touch controls; pause menu; saving in the browser; KayKit models throughout except predators, scarecrow and eggs; real audio and music.

## Next
- Real animals for the predators: fox, hawk and snake are still primitives. No free KayKit pack has them; neither do the paid ones, going by their descriptions.
- Scarecrow and hay bale models (also missing from KayKit).
- More to place: paths, ponds, troughs, nest boxes, sheds, trees and hedges you plant yourself.
- Hen breeds with different looks and egg colours; chicks inherit from parents.
- A reason to keep eggs beyond buying things: orders from neighbours, a market day.
- Seasons and weather (Forest EXTRA and Medieval Hexagon EXTRA have seasonal colour variants).
- Feeders you fill by hand as an alternative to feed patches.
- Rename hens; pick them up and carry them.
- Farmer customisation (KayKit Series 6 has two farmer characters).
- Fence placement: drag to paint a run instead of one piece per tap.
- Save export or cloud save (today it is per browser only).
- Pause menu for Come By.
