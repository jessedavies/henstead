How the farm works

  • A hen lays each morning if she was fed, slept in a coop and stayed calm. Tap a hen to see how she is doing.
  • Three hens can live on grass. More need feed patches or cleared land.
  • At dusk hens walk home to a coop with room. Leave a gate open for them.
  • Eggs are your money: spend them in the build menu.

Your farm saves automatically in this browser.

Henstead

A small chicken farm in a forest clearing. There is no clock to beat and nothing to lose.

Walk with WASD or click the ground. Press E or click things to use them.Tap the ground to walk, or hold and drag to steer. Tap things to use them. Pinch to zoom. The to-do card in the corner shows what to try next, and a yellow arrow points at it.
