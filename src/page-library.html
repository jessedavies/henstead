01 · Look development

Why the models don't feel polished yet

The models are fine; the lighting flattens them. Switch between the game's current setup and the proposed one, then try each change on its own.

Tone

No tone mapping

The game renders linear light straight to the screen, so bright sun clips to flat colour and the soft gradients baked into the KayKit atlases wash out. ACES tone mapping with a warmer, lower sun brings them back.

Grounding

Nothing touches the ground

Hard PCF shadows and no ambient occlusion make props look pasted on. Soft shadows from a lower, warmer sun give every hen, rock and fence post a readable contact shadow, which is most of the "toy diorama" feel in KayKit's own renders. Screen-space AO helps on desktop but is too heavy for iPad and shows artifacts, so it's off in the proposal.

Life

Everything is still

Hens slide, trees freeze. A little squash-and-bob on idle animals, a breeze in the trees and butterflies over the flowers make the clearing feel alive at almost no cost.

Trees

Why trees vanish

It isn't render distance. fadeTrees() shrinks every tree in a wide wedge between the camera and the farmer to 10% size, so trees you're looking at pop away. Fix: fade (dither) only trees that actually cover the farmer or a hen, and never scale them.

06 · UI kit

A UI made of the same stuff as the world

The current HUD is dark translucent slate: the default look of a web app, not of a cosy farm. The proposal uses paper, wood and the KayKit palette, chunky buttons with a pressed lip, and icons rendered from the game's own 3D models, so the interface and the world read as one thing.

07 · Direction

Clearing should uncover things

Clearing is a chore with no payoff beyond space. The smallest change that fixes it reuses what exists: give some bushes, rocks and stumps something hidden underneath, and give finds somewhere to live.
